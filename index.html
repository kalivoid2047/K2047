About

THE PERSON BEHIND THE SYSTEMS

Kylan 2047 is the studio and brand of Kali, a developer who builds intelligent systems end to end: a private personal AI assistant, a peer-to-peer messaging app, a 3D car paint studio and an automotive diagnostics tester.

Different fields, one habit: start with a clear idea, build it as a system that can grow, and measure the impact. The name looks forward to 2047, the future these systems are being built for.

      Kylan AI · Phase 2

      Memory you control

      Kylan's memory starts in "ask" mode: it proposes what it learned, and uses nothing until you accept it. Say "remember that…" and it stores the fact directly.

      Incognito conversations neither read nor write memory, and the Core makes a verified backup of its database every day. Phase 2 is implemented; the exit criteria are still pending.

      Tiff Tester Pro · v0.3.0

      Two brains are safer than one

      The tester pairs an ESP32 main controller with an independent Arduino Nano safety controller, so a fault in one cannot silently drive the outputs. CAN and K-Line drivers, a BLE service and a Flutter app are all in place.

      It is still a bench prototype: nothing has been validated end to end on real hardware, and the injector and coil driver hardware doesn't exist yet.

      Color Simulator · Phase 1

      The 3D paint studio works

      Pick a vehicle, orbit it, paint panels with solid, metallic, matte, gloss, pearlescent and chrome finishes, project decals across seams, swap wheels, and save builds to a garage. Undo and redo are real history, and you can export a 2× PNG.

      Accounts, licensed vehicles, the workshop environment and the career mode are designed but not built yet.
